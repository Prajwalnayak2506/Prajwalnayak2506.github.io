About

The longer version: how I got here, what I love, and what I want to do next.

Hello

I’m Prajwal, a final-year Dual Degree (B.Tech + M.Tech) student in Electrical Engineering at IIT Bombay, specializing in communication and signal processing, with a minor in AI and Data Science.

I like problems where lots of people share one system and the design has to work for all of them: a neighbourhood’s batteries, a mechanism people might try to game, a voice squeezed into almost no data.

When I’m not doing that, I’m probably on a trek, rewatching Your Name, or trying to befriend the nearest dog.

My story

How I got here
Before IITB

Topper, then not so sure

I was the class topper until 8th grade, and I stayed the school topper right to the end. Somewhere around 10th grade I figured out I was queer, and school got harder. Friends stopped talking to me, I was bullied towards the end, and I started doubting myself a lot.

For 11th and 12th I prepared for NEET, and it was honestly going well. Then I met a boy from the JEE batch. We studied together, I fell for him, and a year in, I switched my entire career plan to JEE. Not my most rational decision. Somehow, I cracked JEE Advanced anyway and ended up at IIT Bombay.

First year

Chemistry and I had a falling out

I started in Environmental Science and Engineering and quickly realized I could not stand chemistry. (We’ve since made peace.) A first-year course, MS 101, pulled me towards electrical engineering, and so did a talk on how research works by a student group called Empower. I spent that year visiting labs in the biotech and electrical engineering departments to see what research actually looked like, and then got a branch change to Electrical Engineering, in the top 7% of my batch.

Later

How power systems won me over

For a long time I was all about ML and signal processing, and power systems felt like the boring corner of EE. Then I took Energy Data Analytics with Prof. Anupama Kowli, and it showed me power systems as data, decisions, and people. I’ve been hooked since, and it led to my first paper.

Dreams

The PhD
Next

What I want from a PhD

Mostly, I want to work on something with real impact. So far I’ve deliberately gone wide, picking up breadth with some depth in energy systems, speech coding, game theory, and machine learning, and I’m still adding to it.

  • Energy systems and data-driven optimization: where my paper is, and where working with data and pulling insights out of it is genuinely fun.
  • Speech coding: my Master’s thesis, which is giving great results right now.
  • Tokenizers: I’m currently working on optimal tokenizers for multiple Indian languages.
  • Game theory: where my facility location project lives.
  • Maybe controls: I don’t have a strong background in it yet, but I’m open to learning.

What I’d rather not do: analog or semiconductor research, EM waves, or radar. And while I love coding and solving real problems, grinding a million DSA questions for placements is the fastest way to make me hate a subject.
