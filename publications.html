EI.A 2026

Assessing Data Center Integration in Indian Residential Renewable Energy Community

P. Nayak, G. S. Kalojiya, A. Kowli. Energy Informatics Academy Conference (EI.A 2026), Beijing. Springer.

Read the paper on Springer

  • Engineered an LP-based dispatch framework in Pyomo (HiGHS) with rule-based peer-to-peer allocation to evaluate energy configurations across 1,833 households and the IIT Bombay data center.
  • Showed that storage architecture outweighs energy sharing, with centralized storage reducing annual grid imports by 63.16% against a grid-only baseline.
  • Found that energy sharing is most impactful in decentralized setups, where cost-effective data center batteries displace residential storage.
