SolarCast: solar power generation forecasting

Energy Data Analytics course project, with Prof. Anupama Kowli

Feb – Mar 2026 · EE619

BootSAR: 10-bit 200 MS/s differential SAR ADC

Mixed-Signal VLSI Design course project

  • Designed a differential bootstrapped switch in 45 nm CMOS, reaching 11.82-bit ENOB and 72.97 dB SNR at 200 MS/s.
  • Sized hold capacitors and a split-capacitor CDAC using thermal-noise and settling constraints for 10-bit accuracy.
  • Implemented asynchronous SAR logic and CRS switching to reduce average DAC switching energy.
  • Characterized the integrated ADC with an FFT, reaching 62.475 dB SNDR and 10.08-bit ENOB.
  • Analog design
  • 45 nm CMOS
  • SAR ADC
